# Shop outreach — email template

Purpose: turn "external links: 0" into real links from the shops that are
already listed. The ask is small and honest: here is your page, feel free to
link to it with a badge. No payment, no obligation, no removal threat.

Send in batches of ~50 from Fernando's own address (not a bulk tool at first
— replies matter, and a founder's address lands better than a noreply).
Start with the highest-reviewed shops; they have the best websites and the
most to gain from a visible listing. Track replies in the CSV's last column.

Contacts come from `npx tsx scripts/extract-contact-emails.ts` →
`outreach/shop-contacts-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` (git-ignored).

---

## English (default)

**Subject:** Your shop is listed on WinterStores — here's your page

Hi,

I run WinterStores (winterstores.co), a free directory that helps skiers
compare rental and gear shops near 80+ resorts. {{shop_name}} is listed —
here's your page:

https://winterstores.co/store/{{slug}}

Two quick things, both optional:

1. If anything on it is wrong or missing (services like boot fitting or
   repairs, opening hours, website), reply and I'll fix it the same day.
2. If you'd like, there's a small "Listed on WinterStores" badge you can put
   on your site that links to your listing: winterstores.co/for-shops

Listing is free and stays free; nothing on the page is paid placement.

Best,
Fernando Granata
WinterStores — winterstores.co
